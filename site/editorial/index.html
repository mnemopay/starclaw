Controls outside the model
Instructions can drift.
The policy still applies.
StarClaw assumes hostile content can steer a model. Its controls govern what that model can do next.

    
01
Execution is isolated. Generated code reaches files and the network through brokered tools.
02
Permissions are explicit. Policies allow, ask, or deny. Untrusted content can escalate sensitive actions to approval.
03
Records are verifiable. Hash chains and signatures make edits detectable when the signing key remains secret.
